Who this is for

This guide is for founders and builders shipping with tools like Lovable, Cursor, v0, Bolt, or Replit — people whose app works, whose users are real, and who haven’t brought in a security engineer yet.

  • You have real users, not a demo audience.
  • You store their data — emails, orders, uploads, messages.
  • You are about to launch, or fundraise, and cannot afford a breach headline first.

What this guide will not do

A self-check finds the common holes — the ones strangers actually walk through. It looks at your app from the outside, as an anonymous visitor; anything that exists only behind a login sits outside it. And it is not a guarantee, and not an audit: a clean pass means these twelve checks found nothing on the day you ran them.

About this guide

This guide is general educational information about common security mistakes in AI-built applications. It is not a professional security audit, and working through it is not a substitute for one: it covers the checks listed in it, on the day you run them, and it cannot find everything. A clean result means these checks found nothing — it is not a guarantee or a certification that your application is secure, and ShipAudit Lab and Probcapital LLC accept no liability for the outcome of checks you run yourself or for decisions you make based on this guide. If your application handles payments, health data or other people's sensitive information, have it reviewed professionally before you rely on it.

The statistics in this guide are drawn from the published studies named next to them.
