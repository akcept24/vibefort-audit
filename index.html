05 — QUESTIONS

The details,
plainly.

Is it legal to scan an app?

Only scan a site you own or have explicit permission to test. You must confirm permission before starting.

What do you need from me?

Your app URL and, for authenticated testing, two test accounts with appropriate roles. We’ll agree on the scope before testing.

How long does an audit take?

A Quick Audit takes 3 days. Timing for Full and Deep audits depends on the scope and is agreed in the brief.

What is in the report?

Each finding includes severity, business impact, evidence, and a practical fix. Findings receive a second independent verification pass.

How does the retest work?

Full and Deep audits include a retest of the reported fixes within 30 days.

What will you never do?

We do not touch production data. The free surface scan does not log in or extract data.
